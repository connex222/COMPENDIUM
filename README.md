# The Compendium

Seven puzzle volumes in one place, including Causeway (word ladder), Reckoner (number grid), Skein (word snake) and Winnow (categories).

**Open it:** https://connex222.github.io/COMPENDIUM/

## How to use it

1. Open the link above in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on a phone, tablet or computer.
2. On a phone, use the browser's **Add to Home Screen** option to keep it one tap away.

Or download `index.html` and open it straight from your device. Everything is in that one file.

## Saving

Your progress and theme are saved on the device you play on. Your data is not sent anywhere. Clearing the browser's data deletes it.
